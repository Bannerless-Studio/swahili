## Fix wave 2 (2026-09-29, re-QA follow-up)

Scope: every fix in the re-QA (verdict NO-SHIP), then a second strict read of all A2 and B1 corpus sentences, a self-check, a third read of both levels after the self-check failed, and a read of every sentence each rebuild brought in until a rebuild brought none. Rules are in vocab-engine langs/sw.py, branch engine-sw (922e8fe, dda1561, 5a28dc1), each with positive and negative tests.

Rules added:
- kuwa: verb, conjunction "that/since" or "kwa kuwa" "because", read from the clause to its right.
- ndiyo/ndio: clause-initial before a noun, or after a comma, is the focus copula. "Yes" stands alone or opens a finite clause.
- A noun that spells a verb stem (jibu, tafsiri) is the imperative before a manner adverb or badala.
- kutoka after an exit noun (njia ya kutoka) is the infinitive. A bare "Wako," closing a letter is -ako "yours".
- taratibu before a verb whose subject is not n-class plural is the adverb. ha-/si- + ja + wa is kuwa, not jaa.
